# INC-2025-016 M-10 conveyor trip — housing wedged under a side guide (not slippage)

SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT (FabPilot demo). Author: shift technician, T.B.

## 1. Summary

The Line 3 assembly conveyor M-10 tripped on its overload relay. Because the roller speed had
dropped before the trip, slippage was suspected at first (it had happened on this conveyor before).
But the current had **risen**, not fallen. A plastic housing was wedged under a side guide.
Removed; no damage.

## 2. Timeline

- 10:14: `drive_roller_speed` falling, `CURR-HIGH` on `drive_motor_current` (4.9 A).
- 10:15: `CURR-CRIT` (5.4 A), `RPM-LOW`, then `TRIP`.
- 10:20: operator suspects the belt; maintenance called.
- 10:35: LOTO, belt walked: housing wedged under the side guide near the tail. Removed.
- 10:50: restarted; normal current 2.4 A.

## 3. Evidence

- Speed falling **and current rising**, within one minute, ending in a trip: a jam
  (MAN-CNV-001 §5.2). Slippage lowers the current and oscillates.
- No oscillation in the speed before the event.
- `gearbox_vibration` normal.

## 4. Root cause

A part fell from the assembly fixture onto the belt edge and wedged under the guide.

## 5. Corrective actions

- Part removed; fixture catch tray extended.
- Gap under the side guide reduced so parts can't wedge.

## 6. Lessons learned

Speed down + current up = jam; speed oscillating + current down = slippage. Same alarm (`RPM-LOW`),
opposite causes.
