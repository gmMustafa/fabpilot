# INC-2025-004 M-07 new belt stretched — 100-hour re-tension missed

SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT (FabPilot demo). Author: maintenance planner, R.S.

## 1. Summary

Three weeks after a new belt was fitted on the Line 2 parts conveyor M-07, the roller speed started
to oscillate under load. The belt had stretched as new belts do; the 100-hour re-tension had been
forgotten because the work order was closed with the belt installation. Re-tensioned.

## 2. Timeline

- 24 Feb: new belt installed and tensioned.
- 14–17 Mar: increasing `RPM-LOW` alarms on `drive_roller_speed` when the molders' parts boxes are
  emptied onto the belt (peak load).
- 18 Mar: elongation measured at 0.12 % (target 0.3 %); re-tensioned per SOP-CNV-008.

## 3. Evidence

- Roller speed oscillating and dipping at peak load only; current dipping with it.
- No contamination on the belt; lagging in good condition.
- Elongation far below target.

## 4. Root cause

Initial stretch of the new belt; the follow-up re-tension was not planned.

## 5. Corrective actions

- Re-tensioned; follow-up re-tension is now created automatically as a separate work order when a
  belt is replaced.

## 6. Lessons learned

The fix belongs in the planning system, not in someone's memory: a belt replacement is two work
orders, not one (MAN-CNV-001 §6).
