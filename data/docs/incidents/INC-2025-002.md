# INC-2025-002 M-06 short shots with no alarm — heater band failed in zone 3

SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT (FabPilot demo). Author: process technician, L.K.

## 1. Summary

Quality control rejected parts from M-06 for short shots and unmelted particles. No alarm on the
machine. The monitored zone 2 temperature was within its band. A clamp ammeter showed no current in
the zone 3 heater band. Band replaced.

## 2. Timeline

- 13 Jan, late shift: first short shots found by quality control.
- 14 Jan, 06:30: machine data checked: no alarms; `barrel_temp_zone2` 205–210 °C (lower part of
  the normal band, usually 220).
- 14 Jan, 07:15: clamp ammeter on all heater bands: zone 3 draws 0 A. Band open-circuit.
- 14 Jan, 08:30: band replaced; zone temperatures back to set point; parts OK.

## 3. Evidence

- Part defects without any alarm. Temperatures have **no LOW alarm** on this machine
  (MAN-IMM-001 §3), so a cold zone stays silent.
- `barrel_temp_zone2` lower than usual but inside its band: zone 2 was losing heat to the cold zone 3.
- `pump_motor_current` slightly higher during plasticizing (colder melt).

## 4. Root cause

End-of-life heater band (open circuit).

## 5. Corrective actions

- Band replaced; monthly heater band check with the clamp ammeter (MAN-IMM-001 §7) enforced.
- Proposal: add a heater current monitor per zone.

## 6. Lessons learned

No alarm does not mean no problem. Some faults only show up in the product, and the monitoring
system can only see what has a sensor.
