# INC-2023-004 M-01 spindle bearing failure after ignored vibration trend

SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT (FabPilot demo). Author: maintenance planner, R.S.

## 1. Summary

Machining Center 1 (M-01) had to be stopped on 14 June 2023 with `TEMP-CRIT` on the spindle bearing.
The front spindle bearing was badly worn. The vibration warnings that announced it had been
acknowledged on the panel for four days without anyone looking at the trend. Unplanned downtime:
2.5 shifts, of which most was waiting for the exchange spindle.

## 2. Timeline

- 10 Jun, early shift: first `VIB-HIGH` on `spindle_vibration` (2.9 mm/s, limit 2.8). Acknowledged.
- 10–13 Jun: `VIB-HIGH` several times per shift, each time acknowledged. Values climbing, 3.1 → 3.6 mm/s.
- 13 Jun, late shift: first `VIB-CRIT` (3.5 mm/s). Operator reported "spindle a bit loud at low speed".
- 14 Jun, 07:40: `TEMP-HIGH` on `spindle_bearing_temp` (56 °C), 09:15 `TEMP-CRIT` (63 °C). Machine stopped.

## 3. Evidence

- 7-day trend: `spindle_vibration` flat around 1.2 mm/s until 9 June, then rising, faster each day.
- `spindle_bearing_temp` normal (40–50 °C) until the morning of 14 June — temperature came **last**.
- `spindle_motor_current` and `coolant_pressure` normal throughout.
- Removed unit: spalling on the outer race of the front bearing, grease dark and dry.

## 4. Root cause

Wear of the front spindle bearing (fatigue spalling, grease at end of life after ~19 000 h). The
fault itself was normal end-of-life wear. The **incident** was that the four days of warnings were
treated as nuisance alarms.

## 5. Corrective actions

- Spindle unit exchanged per SOP-CNC-002; run-in passed, 0.8 mm/s at 8 000 rpm.
- Repeated `VIB-HIGH` on the same signal within 24 h now creates a maintenance notification
  automatically instead of only a panel alarm.
- Weekly vibration trend review added to the maintenance plan (MAN-CNC-001 §7).

## 6. Lessons learned

Bearing wear announces itself days in advance in the vibration trend, and only at the end in the
temperature. By the time the temperature alarms, the planned repair has become an unplanned one.
