# INC-2023-007 M-09 high vibration right after a motor repair — soft foot and misalignment

SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT (FabPilot demo). Author: reliability technician, A.D.

## 1. Summary

After the M-09 motor came back from rewinding, `VIB-HIGH` appeared immediately on the first start.
A bearing problem was suspected because the motor had just been worked on. The vibration had not
grown over time: it was there from the first minute. One motor foot was not sitting flat (soft
foot). Shimmed and re-tightened; vibration normal.

## 2. Timeline

- 4 Sep, 14:00: motor refitted after rewinding.
- 4 Sep, 14:20: first start: `bearing_vibration` 3.2 mm/s, `VIB-HIGH`.
- 4 Sep, 15:00: bearing noise check: no bearing noise. Spectrum: dominant at running speed (1×),
  not at bearing frequencies.
- 5 Sep, 08:00: base bolts checked with a dial indicator: one foot lifted 0.25 mm when loosened.
  Shimmed, re-tightened: 0.9 mm/s.

## 3. Evidence

- Vibration high **from the first start**, constant, not rising over days. Bearing wear builds up
  gradually (MAN-PMP-001 §5.2).
- `bearing_temp` normal.
- Spectrum at 1× running speed (typical of looseness and misalignment).

## 4. Root cause

Soft foot: the motor base had not been checked when the motor was refitted.

## 5. Corrective actions

- Shims fitted; soft-foot check added to the motor refit checklist.

## 6. Lessons learned

The time pattern decides it: wear grows, assembly errors are there from the start. Not every
vibration alarm on a pump is a bearing.
