# INC-2024-016 False alarm: M-06 hydraulic oil temperature rising on one machine only

SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT (FabPilot demo). Author: instrumentation technician, S.L.

## 1. Summary

`TEMP-HIGH` on the hydraulic oil temperature of M-06 after a rise over two days. The first plan was
to descale the oil cooler. M-05, on the same cooling water, was normal; M-06's pump current and
pressures were normal too. Reference thermometer in the tank: 46 °C, sensor: 57 °C. Sensor replaced.

## 2. Timeline

- 12–14 Oct: `hydraulic_oil_temp` on M-06 creeping up, also over the weekend with the machine off.
- 14 Oct: `TEMP-HIGH` (56 °C). Cooler descaling planned.
- 15 Oct: SOP-GEN-004 desk check: M-05 oil temperature normal; cooling water supply from M-09
  normal; M-06 `pump_motor_current` and `hydraulic_pressure` unchanged.
- 15 Oct: reference measurement 46 °C; sensor head with water inside. Replaced.

## 3. Evidence

- One machine, one signal. A cooling water problem would affect both molders (MAN-IMM-001 §5.4); a
  fouled cooler would rise over weeks and follow the load.
- The reading rose at the weekend with the hydraulics off.
- 11 °C difference to the reference.

## 4. Root cause

Damaged cable gland on the RTD head, water ingress during machine cleaning.

## 5. Corrective actions

- Sensor replaced; gland checked on M-05 as well. Cooler descaling cancelled.

## 6. Lessons learned

Comparing with the twin machine on the same supply answered the question in five minutes.
