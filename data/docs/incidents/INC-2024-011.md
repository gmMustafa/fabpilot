# INC-2024-011 M-01 coolant pump motor burnt out, spindle restarted too hot

SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT (FabPilot demo). Author: maintenance supervisor, J.W.

## 1. Summary

The coolant pump of M-01 failed in the night shift: coolant pressure dropped to zero, the spindle
heated up and the coolant interlock tripped the machine. The operator reset and restarted twice
before maintenance arrived. Pump motor winding burnt (coolant in the terminal box). Pump replaced.
The spindle survived but needed a full run-in.

## 2. Timeline

- 02:18: `PRESS-LOW` on `coolant_pressure`; pressure at 0.2 bar within a minute.
- 02:29: `TEMP-HIGH` on `spindle_bearing_temp`, 02:31 `TRIP`.
- 02:40 and 02:55: restarted by the operator, each time `TRIP` within minutes.
- 03:20: maintenance on site; insulation resistance of the pump motor 0.05 MΩ.
- 06:00: exchange pump fitted (SOP-CNC-003).

## 3. Evidence

- Coolant pressure collapse within a minute, then spindle temperature rise, then trip.
- Terminal box of the pump: coolant inside, cable gland not tight.
- Insulation resistance far below 1 MΩ.

## 4. Root cause

Coolant entered the pump motor terminal box through a loose cable gland; the winding insulation
broke down.

## 5. Corrective actions

- Pump replaced; gland sealed; all three machining centers checked.
- Spindle run-in repeated (SOP-CNC-002 step 13); vibration 1.1 mm/s, accepted.
- Operators briefed: after a coolant `TRIP`, no restart without maintenance.

## 6. Lessons learned

Restarting a spindle that has just run dry and hot, onto cold coolant, risks the spindle bearings.
A coolant trip is not a nuisance alarm.
