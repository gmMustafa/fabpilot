# INC-2026-007 False alarm: M-10 drive motor temperature high at normal load

SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT (FabPilot demo). Author: electrician, P.H.

## 1. Summary

`TEMP-HIGH` on the drive motor temperature of the assembly conveyor M-10. With the slippage history
of this conveyor (INC-2026-002) the first thought was an overloaded drive. But current, speed and
gearbox vibration were all normal, and the motor housing was cool to the touch. A loose terminal in
the junction box had shifted the reading. Terminal re-tightened.

## 2. Timeline

- 6–8 Jun: `drive_motor_temp` rising slowly, from 45 to 71 °C, regardless of the load.
- 8 Jun: `TEMP-HIGH` (71 °C).
- 9 Jun, 07:00: housing measured with a contact thermometer: 44 °C. `drive_motor_current` 2.6 A
  (normal), `drive_roller_speed` steady.
- 9 Jun, 07:40: junction box: loose sensor terminal, oxidized. Cleaned and tightened; reading 44 °C.

## 3. Evidence

- The temperature rose without any change in current. A real overload heats the motor because it
  draws more current (SOP-GEN-007 §4.1).
- The reading did not follow the load cycles of the line.
- Reference measurement 27 °C lower.

## 4. Root cause

Loose, oxidized terminal on the temperature sensor circuit, adding resistance.

## 5. Corrective actions

- Terminal cleaned and tightened; all junction boxes on M-04, M-07, M-10 checked.

## 6. Lessons learned

A machine's history makes some explanations feel likely. Check the other signals before following
the history.
