# INC-2024-005 M-08 inlet filter clogged in three weeks during construction work

SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT (FabPilot demo). Author: utilities technician, M.F.

## 1. Summary

The inlet filter of the screw compressor M-08 reached its alarm limit only three weeks after a
scheduled change (normal interval 2 000 h, about 4 months). Cause: concrete dust from the hall
extension. Element replaced; temporary pre-filter mat fitted for the construction period.

## 2. Timeline

- 30 Apr: element changed per schedule; `inlet_filter_dp` 18 mbar.
- 14–19 May: dp rising steadily, about 2 mbar per day.
- 20 May: `PRESS-HIGH` on `inlet_filter_dp` (52 mbar). `discharge_temp` at 93 °C, higher than usual.
- 21 May: element replaced during the lunch break (SOP-CMP-005); dp 17 mbar.

## 3. Evidence

- `inlet_filter_dp` rising gradually over days, not jumping.
- `discharge_temp` and `motor_current` slightly higher in the same period: the filter signature of
  three signals moving together (MAN-CMP-001 §6).
- Element covered with grey, fine mineral dust.

## 4. Root cause

Construction dust from the neighbouring hall extension, drawn in through the compressor room's
ventilation louvres.

## 5. Corrective actions

- Element replaced; pre-filter mat on the louvres until the construction ended.
- `inlet_filter_dp` checked daily during the construction period.

## 6. Lessons learned

A filter interval is valid for normal air. Any dusty work near the compressor room shortens it; ask
facility management about construction before planning filter stock.
