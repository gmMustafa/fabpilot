# INC-2025-017 M-06 overload after a mold change — barrel profile not adjusted, reset three times

SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT (FabPilot demo). Author: maintenance supervisor, J.W.

## 1. Summary

After a mold and material change on M-06 (PP to ABS), the barrel profile stayed at the PP settings.
The plasticizing drive was overloaded, the relay tripped and was reset three times by different
people within two hours. The third reset ended in a damaged check ring. 1.5 days downtime.

## 2. Timeline

- 06:00: mold and material change finished; machine started.
- 06:40–07:30: `CURR-HIGH` on `pump_motor_current` in episodes, `screw_speed` dropping,
  `RPM-LOW` twice.
- 07:35, 08:05, 08:40: three `TRIP`s, each followed by a reset.
- 09:00: screw stalls; maintenance called. Check ring found broken on disassembly the next day.

## 3. Evidence

- The overload signature (current up, speed down) right from the start of the new material.
- Barrel set points 20–25 °C below the ABS data sheet.
- No mechanical problem found before the third trip.

## 4. Root cause

Wrong barrel profile after the material change (missing step in the change checklist). The repeated
resets without investigation turned a set-point error into a mechanical failure.

## 5. Corrective actions

- Check ring replaced; screw inspected (no damage).
- Material change checklist: barrel profile loaded from the material data record, signed off.
- SOP-GEN-007 rule enforced: after a second trip, the panel requires a maintenance key to reset.

## 6. Lessons learned

Three resets in two hours is the pattern SOP-GEN-007 exists for. Each person thought "one reset is
fine" without knowing about the others.
