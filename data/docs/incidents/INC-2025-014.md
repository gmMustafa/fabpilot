# INC-2025-014 M-02 coolant loss — sheared pump coupling

SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT (FabPilot demo). Author: shift technician, K.M.

## 1. Summary

Coolant pressure on M-02 fell to zero within a minute and the machine tripped on the coolant
interlock. The pump motor was running, so the first idea was a blocked suction. The coupling
between motor and pump shaft had sheared. Downtime 2 h.

## 2. Timeline

- 10:47: `PRESS-LOW` on `coolant_pressure` (0.3 bar).
- 10:58: `TEMP-HIGH` on `spindle_bearing_temp` (56 °C).
- 11:00: `TRIP` (coolant flow interlock).
- 11:10: pump motor heard running. Tank level and filter basket fine.
- 11:40: pump lifted: coupling insert broken; motor shaft turning, pump shaft not.
- 12:45: coupling replaced, pressure 4.4 bar, machine released.

## 3. Evidence

- Sudden pressure loss (seconds to a minute), then temperature rise, then trip.
- Motor running but drawing less current than usual: no hydraulic load.
- Level and basket normal, which excluded the slow causes.

## 4. Root cause

Fatigue failure of the elastomer coupling insert (original part, 8 years old).

## 5. Corrective actions

- Coupling insert replaced on M-02; inserts on M-01 and M-03 inspected, M-01 replaced as well.
- Coupling insert added to the 6-monthly pump inspection (SOP-CNC-003).

## 6. Lessons learned

"Motor running" does not mean "pump pumping". The same symptom pattern as INC-2023-009 had a
different mechanical cause; the diagnosis is the same: pressure collapse first.
