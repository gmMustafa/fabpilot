# INC-2024-008 M-09 low discharge pressure after pipe work — strainer full of welding beads

SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT (FabPilot demo). Author: utilities technician, M.F.

## 1. Summary

Two days after a contractor extended the cooling-water circuit, the strainer dp of M-09 rose and the
discharge pressure fell until `PRESS-LOW` was raised. On Line 2 the hydraulic oil temperature of both
molders went up. Strainer full of welding beads and scale. Cleaned; circuit flushed.

## 2. Timeline

- 28 Jun: pipe work on the circuit finished, system refilled.
- 29 Jun–1 Jul: `suction_strainer_dp` rising from 0.10 to 0.34 bar; `PRESS-HIGH` on it on 1 Jul.
- 1 Jul, night: `PRESS-LOW` on `discharge_pressure` (2.4 bar).
- 2 Jul, morning: Line 2 reports oil temperatures near the limit on M-05 and M-06.
- 2 Jul, 09:00: strainer cleaned (SOP-PMP-006): about 1 kg of welding beads and scale.

## 3. Evidence

- Strainer dp rising over days while discharge pressure fell.
- `motor_current` slightly lower than usual (less flow).
- Both molders affected at the same time: a shared cause (cooling water), see MAN-IMM-001 §5.4.

## 4. Root cause

Debris from the pipe work. The contractor had not flushed the new section before connecting it.

## 5. Corrective actions

- Strainer cleaned, cleaned again after 3 days (0.3 kg more).
- Contract terms updated: flushing through a temporary strainer before connection.

## 6. Lessons learned

Any work on the circuit means strainer checks for the following week. The basket contents named the
cause immediately.
