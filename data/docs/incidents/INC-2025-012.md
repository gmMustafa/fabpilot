# INC-2025-012 False alarm: M-09 strainer dp high, but the basket was clean

SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT (FabPilot demo). Author: utilities technician, M.F.

## 1. Summary

`PRESS-HIGH` on `suction_strainer_dp` of M-09 after a slow rise. The strainer was opened, cooling
water for Line 2 interrupted for 30 minutes, and the basket was clean. The transmitter showed
0.20 bar with the pump stopped (should be about 0). Transmitter re-zeroed, then replaced.

## 2. Timeline

- 3–6 Oct: `suction_strainer_dp` slowly rising, 0.12 → 0.31 bar.
- 6 Oct, 15:00: `PRESS-HIGH`. Strainer cleaning planned for the next morning.
- 7 Oct, 07:00: pump stopped, strainer opened: basket clean. Reading at standstill: 0.20 bar (normal running dp with a clean basket is about 0.10).
- 7 Oct, 08:30: transmitter checked against a test gauge (SOP-GEN-004): zero offset confirmed.

## 3. Evidence

- `discharge_pressure` and `motor_current` normal for the whole period. A clogged strainer lowers
  both (MAN-PMP-001 §5.1, §5.4); they did not move.
- Non-zero dp reading with the pump stopped.
- Clean basket.

## 4. Root cause

Water froze in the transmitter's impulse lines during a frost night in the unheated pump room; the
sensor diaphragm was stressed and the zero shifted.

## 5. Corrective actions

- Transmitter replaced; impulse lines insulated and trace-heated.
- Desk check per SOP-GEN-004 §4.1 made mandatory before opening the strainer.

## 6. Lessons learned

Opening the strainer cost 30 minutes of Line 2 cooling water for nothing. The standstill reading and
the unchanged discharge pressure would have shown it from the desk.
