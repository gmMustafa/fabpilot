# INC-2024-003 M-04 outfeed conveyor slipping — coolant mist on the belt

SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT (FabPilot demo). Author: shift technician, T.B.

## 1. Summary

The Line 1 outfeed conveyor M-04 showed an oscillating roller speed with dips below the limit, and
parts arrived late at the end of the line. The belt underside and the drive roller were wet with
coolant emulsion from the machining centers. Belt and roller cleaned, mist guard fitted.

## 2. Timeline

- 11 Mar: operators report "conveyor jerking".
- 11 Mar, afternoon: `RPM-LOW` on `drive_roller_speed` (82 rpm), several times, clearing after
  a minute or two.
- 12 Mar, 06:00: inspection after LOTO: belt underside and drive roller lagging greasy with emulsion.
- 12 Mar, 07:30: cleaned, tension checked (within specification), released (SOP-CNV-008).

## 3. Evidence

- `drive_roller_speed` oscillating with a period of about 2 minutes, dipping below 85 rpm.
- `drive_motor_current` fluctuating with it and **lower** than usual: less torque transmitted
  (MAN-CNV-001 §5.1). A jam would have raised the current.
- `gearbox_vibration` normal.

## 4. Root cause

Coolant mist from the open machining-center doors drifting onto the conveyor, which runs right next
to them.

## 5. Corrective actions

- Belt and roller cleaned; mist guard fitted between the machines and the conveyor.
- Belt cleaning added to the weekly plan for M-04.

## 6. Lessons learned

Re-tensioning a contaminated belt only hides the slip for a while. Clean first (SOP-CNV-008 §4.1).
