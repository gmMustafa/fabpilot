# INC-2026-901 False alarm: M-01 coolant pressure reading low, coolant flow normal

SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT (FabPilot demo). Author: maintenance engineer, M.K.

## 1. Summary

`PRESS-LOW` on the coolant pressure of M-01 after the reading had been sinking for three days.
Coolant was flowing normally at the nozzles and the spindle stayed cool. Reference gauge 4.4 bar,
transmitter 2.8 bar. Emulsion inside the transmitter housing; transmitter replaced. No work on the
coolant pump.

## 2. Timeline

- 15–17 Mar: `coolant_pressure` slowly falling, from about 4.5 to 3.1 bar, the same in every program.
- 18 Mar, 06:00: shift start, coolant on.
- 18 Mar, 09:16: `PRESS-LOW` (2.9 bar), after the warm-up hour. Operator pauses the program.
- 09:25: tank level, filter basket and pump checked: all fine, good coolant jets at the nozzles.
- 09:40: test gauge at the nozzle manifold: 4.4 bar. Transmitter: 2.8 bar.
- 10:05: transmitter removed: emulsion inside the housing, cable gland cracked.
- 10:40: new transmitter fitted (SOP-GEN-004 §4.3): 4.4 bar, matches the gauge. Program resumed.

## 3. Evidence

- Reference gauge 4.4 bar against a transmitter reading of 2.8 bar.
- `spindle_bearing_temp` 44–48 °C for the whole period. A real coolant loss heats the spindle within
  minutes (INC-2023-009, INC-2024-011); three days of "low pressure" without any temperature rise
  is physically implausible.
- The pressure fell slowly over days. A failing pump loses pressure within about a minute; a filter
  or level problem over hours, and both would show at the nozzles.
- `spindle_vibration` and `spindle_motor_current` unchanged.

## 4. Root cause

Cracked cable gland on the transmitter (probably from a hose hitting it during a tank cleaning).
Emulsion reached the measuring cell and shifted the reading down.

## 5. Corrective actions

- Transmitter replaced; cable gland replaced with a metal one.
- Glands of the coolant transmitters on M-02 and M-03 checked: one more cracked gland on M-02,
  replaced.

## 6. Lessons learned

The spindle temperature was the proof: without coolant, it can't stay at 45 °C. A pressure value
that sinks for days while the machine behaves normally is a measurement question first.
