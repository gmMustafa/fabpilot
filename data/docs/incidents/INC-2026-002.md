# INC-2026-002 M-10 assembly conveyor slipping — drive roller lagging worn smooth

SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT (FabPilot demo). Author: shift technician, K.M.

## 1. Summary

The Line 3 assembly conveyor M-10 was re-tensioned for slippage in October and December 2025. In
January it slipped again. This time the drive roller was inspected: the lagging was worn smooth and
partly loose. Lagging repaired; belt tension back to the normal value.

## 2. Timeline

- Oct 2025 and Dec 2025: slippage, re-tensioned each time (elongation raised to 0.4 %, above target).
- 26 Jan: `RPM-LOW` on `drive_roller_speed` several times per hour, speed oscillating.
- 27 Jan: LOTO, roller inspected: lagging polished, one section detached. Repaired with the lagging
  kit (SOP-CNV-008 §4.1), tension reset to 0.3 %.

## 3. Evidence

- Oscillating roller speed with falling current: slippage, not overload.
- History: two re-tensions within three months — a sign the real cause had not been fixed.
- Over-tensioning to 0.4 % had increased the load on the gearbox bearings (`gearbox_vibration` up
  from 1.1 to 1.6 mm/s since October, still below the limit).

## 4. Root cause

Worn drive roller lagging. Over-tensioning was used as a workaround twice.

## 5. Corrective actions

- Lagging repaired; tension back to specification; gearbox vibration to be re-checked in 4 weeks.
- Lagging inspection added to every slippage work order.

## 6. Lessons learned

When the same fix is needed again and again, the fix is wrong. Over-tensioning moves the problem to
the gearbox.
