# INC-2024-002 M-09 pump bearing wear found early in the weekly trend review

SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT (FabPilot demo). Author: reliability technician, A.D.

## 1. Summary

During the weekly trend review the `bearing_vibration` of the central coolant pump M-09 showed a
steady rise, still below the warning limit. Planned bearing replacement on Saturday; no production
impact. Good catch, recorded as an incident so the pattern is findable.

## 2. Timeline

- 29 Jan: weekly review — vibration 1.6 mm/s (baseline 0.9). Noted, re-check in 3 days.
- 1 Feb: 2.1 mm/s. Handheld spectrum: bearing defect frequency of the drive-end bearing visible.
- 3 Feb: 2.5 mm/s, still no alarm. Replacement scheduled for Saturday 6 Feb with a temporary
  cooling-water pump for Line 2.
- 6 Feb: bearings and mechanical seal replaced (SOP-PMP-009).

## 3. Evidence

- `bearing_vibration` rising over 8 days, accelerating; `VIB-HIGH` never reached (2.8 mm/s).
- `bearing_temp` unchanged (45–50 °C).
- `discharge_pressure`, `motor_current`, `suction_strainer_dp` normal: not a hydraulic problem.
- Removed drive-end bearing 6309: early-stage spalling on the inner race.

## 4. Root cause

Early-stage fatigue of the drive-end bearing after about 24 000 operating hours, in line with the
expected life.

## 5. Corrective actions

- Both bearings and the seal replaced per SOP-PMP-009. After 1 h: 0.8 mm/s, `bearing_temp` 52 °C.
- Spare bearing set re-ordered (min. stock 1).

## 6. Lessons learned

The trend found it a week before the first alarm would have. A vibration value that is still
"normal" but has doubled from its baseline is already information.
